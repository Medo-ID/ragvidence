# Plans and editions

RAGvidence uses two proprietary plans. The plan shown on the purchase receipt controls the license grant and available build.

## Free

The Free edition is generous enough to complete real retrieval investigations:

- local BM25 retrieval;
- optional local semantic retrieval;
- user-owned Ollama and OpenAI-compatible providers;
- body and heading-context experiments;
- custom reviewed datasets;
- imported results from an existing RAG stack;
- evidence-level metrics and regression analysis;
- JSON, Markdown, and self-contained HTML reports;
- optional answer inspection;
- local workspaces, health checks, verification, and recovery.

Free requires no account or activation.

## Plus

Plus is the paid entitlement for advanced workflows distributed in designated Plus builds. A capability belongs to Plus only when it is present in the delivered build and listed on the product page or release notes.

Planned areas include multi-run experiment matrices, advanced retrieval interventions, deeper failure analysis, automated regression gates, and richer exports. Planned items are not promises and are not part of the current Free `0.1.0` release.

## What a purchase does not include

- API credits, hosted models, or vector-database hosting;
- guaranteed retrieval or answer accuracy;
- legal, financial, medical, compliance, or audit advice;
- permission to redistribute, resell, sublicense, or expose the software as a hosted service;
- team seats unless the checkout explicitly says so.

The detailed grants and restrictions are in [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md). Checkout terms and the receipt control price, taxes, billing period, update period, support level, and refund eligibility.
